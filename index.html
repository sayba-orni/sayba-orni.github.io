Research Interests

SIGNAL PROCESSING / MACHINE LEARNING / SENSOR FUSION

Learning from multiple sensors without losing what makes each one useful.

Coming from an EEE background, I would like to work on such problems where I can apply my knowledge of digital signal processing while developing machine learning models for real-world sensing applications. This combination of signal processing and machine learning is where my research interests lie. I am interested in working on research problems such as how we can combine information from different sensors without losing useful information from either one, especially when one sensor is noisy or unreliable.

01 / FOUNDATION

Digital Signal Processing

Applying my EEE background to understand signals, noise, and the information captured by sensors.

02 / METHODS

Machine Learning for Sensing

Developing models that learn useful patterns from real-world sensor measurements.

03 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Robust Sensor Fusion

Exploring how to combine complementary sensor information when a sensor becomes noisy or unreliable.

My medical imaging research and hands-on electronics projects are part of the background I bring to these questions.
